# The Janus System

choosing between your own designs is harder than it looks. the more options you're staring at, the longer it takes to decide — and the less confident you feel about it. that's hick's law. the janus system sidesteps it entirely by breaking the decision down into the simplest possible question: which of these two do you prefer? one pair at a time, no ranking, no scoring, no overthinking. over enough comparisons, an elo rating system — the same one used in chess — surfaces the strongest design naturally. the result isn't just a winner, it's evidence. built for design students, courses, and clubs who want real collective feedback without the chaos of a group critique. free, open source, no account needed.
